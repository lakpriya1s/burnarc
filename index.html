How it works

Set up once. Then just glance.

BurnArc reads the numbers Claude Code already prints to its status line. Nothing to log in to, nothing to paste.

  1. Add your Claude Code profiles

    Open BurnArc on your Mac and pick your profile folders — ~/.claude, ~/.claude-work, as many as you use. One click installs a tiny status-line hook. Your existing status line, ccbar included, keeps working.

  2. Watch the menu bar

    Two live rings and a number — 83% or 83% · 1h32 — follow your tightest limit. Click for every account, window, pace and run-out time.

  3. Pair your iPhone

    Scan a QR code from the Mac. Your iPhone, Apple Watch and widgets pick up the same numbers over your Wi-Fi — no cloud, no account.

Privacy

Your usage stays on your devices.

There's no BurnArc account and no BurnArc server. Ever.

No credentials

BurnArc never sees your Claude password, OAuth token or API key, and never calls Anthropic's APIs on your behalf.

No prompts, no transcripts

Only usage percentages, reset times, your account labels and the live session's model, effort, cost and project folder are shared with your iPhone. Prompts, replies, transcripts and session IDs never leave the Mac.

Local, encrypted sync

Mac to iPhone over your own Wi-Fi with TLS and a per-phone pairing key you can revoke. iPhone to Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity.

Sandboxed and notarized

The Mac app runs in Apple's sandbox, only touches the profile folders you choose, and is notarized by Apple. No analytics, no tracking.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does BurnArc need my Claude login or API key?

No. BurnArc never asks for your Claude password, OAuth token or API key. It reads the usage figures Claude Code already gives its status line, through a small hook in the Claude Code profiles you choose.

Does it work alongside ccbar or my own status line?

Yes. The hook records the status-line data, then runs your existing status line command with the same input, so your terminal looks exactly as before. Uninstalling restores your original setting.

Why does the iPhone app need a Mac?

Claude Code runs on your Mac, so that's where the usage numbers are. The Mac app shares them with the iPhones you pair over your local Wi-Fi, and the iPhone passes them to your Apple Watch. There's no server in between.

Which Claude plans are supported?

Claude Code reports plan limits for Pro and Max subscriptions, so that's where BurnArc shows 5-hour and weekly windows. Other accounts appear with no plan limits reported.

How is the run-out time worked out?

From your average burn since the window started: what's left × time elapsed ÷ what's used — the same estimate as ccbar. It's shown only when it comes before the window resets.

How do I update the Mac app?

Download the latest DMG from this site and drag BurnArc into Applications to replace the old copy. Your settings, accounts and pairings are kept.

Is BurnArc made by Anthropic?

No. BurnArc is an independent app by CodeScale that works with Claude Code. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
